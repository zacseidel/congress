About

Somnigroup International Inc is a bedding company, dedicated to improving people's lives through good sleep. With superior capabilities in design, manufacturing, distribution and retail, It delivers breakthrough sleep solutions and serves the evolving needs of consumers in more than 100 countries world-wide through its fully-owned businesses Mattress Firm, Tempur Sealy North America and Tempur Sealy International.
