About

Invesco provides investment-management services to retail (70% of managed assets) and institutional (30%) clients. At the end of February 2026, the firm had $2.258 trillion in assets under management spread among its equity (62% of AUM), balanced (3%), fixed-income (20%), alternative investment (6%), and money market (9%) operations. Passive products account for close to half of Invesco's total AUM. Invesco's US retail business is one of the 10 largest nonproprietary fund complexes in the country.
