About

vTv Therapeutics Inc is a late-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing oral, small-molecule drug candidates intended to help treat people living with diabetes and other chronic diseases. Its clinical pipeline is led by cadisegliatin (TTP399), a novel, small-molecule, liver-selective glucokinase activator (GKA) currently being evaluated as a potential oral adjunctive therapy to insulin for the treatment of type 1 diabetes (T1D). In addition, the company is also engaged in the research and development of other candidates in its pipeline through collaborations with academic partners PHARMACEUTICAL PREPARATIONS · website
