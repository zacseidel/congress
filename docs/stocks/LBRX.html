About

LB Pharmaceuticals Inc is a late-stage biopharmaceutical company developing novel therapies for the treatment of a wide range of neuropsychiatric disorders including schizophrenia, bipolar depression, adjunctive treatment of depressive disorder and other diseases. It is building a pipeline that leverages the broad therapeutic potential of its product candidate, LB-102, which it believes has the potential to be the first benzamide antipsychotic drug approved for neuropsychiatric disorders in the United States.
