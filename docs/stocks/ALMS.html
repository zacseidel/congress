About

Alumis Inc is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company with an initial focus on developing its two Tyrosine Kinase 2 ("TYK2") inhibitors: envudeucitinib ("envu"), an allosteric TYK2 inhibitor for the treatment of PsO and SLE; and A-005, a central nervous system ("CNS") penetrant allosteric TYK2 inhibitor, to offer the therapeutic benefit of TYK2 inhibition within the CNS for a broad range of neuroinflammatory and neurodegenerative diseases.
