About

Transocean Ltd. is an international provider of offshore contract drilling services for oil and gas wells. The company provides mobile offshore drilling rigs, related equipment, and crews to support the drilling of oil and gas wells. Its fleet mainly consists of offshore rigs, including drillships, semisubmersibles, and jackups. DRILLING OIL & GAS WELLS · website
