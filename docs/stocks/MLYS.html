About

Mineralys Therapeutics Inc is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing medicines to target diseases driven by dysregulated aldosterone. The Company's product candidate, lorundrostat, is a proprietary, orally administered, aldosterone synthase inhibitor that the Company is developing for the treatment of cardiorenal conditions affected by dysregulated aldosterone, including hypertension and related comorbidities, such as chronic kidney disease and obstructive sleep apnea.
