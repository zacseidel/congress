About

EquipmentShare.com Inc is a vertically integrated platform that combines proprietary technology, a connected equipment fleet, and a nationwide footprint to serve the construction industry. The company operates its business through the following reportable segments: Equipment Rental and Services Operations, comprised of recurring activity performed at full-service branch locations, such as equipment rentals and related services, and sales of parts, supplies, and maintenance services to construction contractors and others.
