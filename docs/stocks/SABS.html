About

SAB Biotherapeutics Inc is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing multi-specific, high-potency, human immunoglobulin G (hIgG) to treat and prevent immune and autoimmune disorders. The company's lead product candidate, SAB-142, targets autoimmune Type 1 Diabetes (T1D) with a disease-modifying therapeutic approach that aims to change the T1D treatment paradigm by delaying onset and potentially preventing disease progression of Stage 3 T1D patients.
