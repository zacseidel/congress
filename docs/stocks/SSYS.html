About

Stratasys Ltd offers polymer-based 3D printing solutions for designers, engineers, manufacturers, dental and medical professionals, and others. Its 3D printing systems utilize the firm's patented extrusion-based FDM, inkjet-based PolyJet, powder-bed-based SAF, photopolymer-based P3, and Neo stereolithography technologies to enable the production of prototypes, tools, and manufactured goods directly from 3D CAD files or other 3D content.
