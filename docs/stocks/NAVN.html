About

Navan Inc is engaged in providing an end-to-end, AI-powered software platform for managing business travel and expense (T&E). The company's platform enables users to manage corporate travel bookings, payments, and expense processes, while providing tools for customers to control and optimize spending and for suppliers to access demand. Geographically, the company operates in the United States, the United Kingdom, and other countries, with the majority of its revenue generated from the United States.
