About

Seacoast Banking Corp of Florida is a holding company. The company provides integrated financial services, including commercial and consumer banking, wealth management, and mortgage and insurance services, to customers across Florida through branch, mobile, and online banking solutions. It maintains day-to-day operations, particularly in the areas of operations, treasury management systems, information technology, and security.
