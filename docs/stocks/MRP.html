About

Millrose Properties Inc is a Holding Company. The company, along with its subsidiaries, engages in providing operational and capital solutions for home builders and land development companies to finance the acquisition and development of land assets through its Homesite Option Purchase Platform. The company generates a key portion of its revenue from Option fee revenues and a smaller portion of revenue from Development loan income. REAL ESTATE · website
